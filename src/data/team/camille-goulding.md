Hi\! I’m **Camille Goulding**, and I’m excited to serve as Vice President of the **Elsa England PTA**\!

I’m a mom of four and passionate about creating strong, welcoming communities where families feel connected, supported, and excited to be involved. Professionally, I work as a Program Specialist with the Texas Workforce Commission, and I have more than 10 years of experience in nonprofit leadership, program management, community outreach, fundraising, and event planning. Before joining TWC, I served as a Program Manager with 4th Trimester Arizona, where much of my work centered around bringing people and organizations together to better support families and communities.

Those experiences have taught me how powerful a community can be when people feel welcomed, heard, and given meaningful ways to participate—and that’s something I hope to bring to our PTA.

As Vice President, I’m excited to work alongside our incredible parents, teachers, staff, and PTA volunteers to create fun experiences for our students, support our teachers, strengthen communication with families, and continue building the sense of community that makes Elsa England such a special place.

Most importantly, I’m a fellow Elsa England parent who wants our kids to have an amazing elementary school experience. I’m looking forward to meeting more of our Leopard families this year, so please come say hello when you see me around campus or at a PTA event\!

**Go Leopards\!**
