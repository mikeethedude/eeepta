I’m Vijaya Baliwada, a mom of two boys, both at Elsa England Elementary. Our family moved to Austin 4 years ago and have since made this community our home.
I enjoy meeting people from different walks of life, learning from our shared experiences as parents, and giving back to a community that gives so much to our children. Professionally, I work in product and strategy in the technology industry, bringing people and ideas together to solve problems and drive impact. I’m excited to bring that experience and energy to the PTA and look forward to getting to know more of our Elsa England families!
