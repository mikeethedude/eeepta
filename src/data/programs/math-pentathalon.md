Welcome to the Math Pentathlon Club at Elsa England!
Our PTA is proud to offer the Math Pentathlon Club—an engaging opportunity for students to strengthen their math thinking skills through fun, strategic games.

What is Math Pentathlon?
Math Pentathlon is a nationally recognized program that uses five challenging and fun board games to develop problem-solving, logical reasoning, and strategic thinking. The games are designed for different grade levels and align with national math standards, making learning both educational and enjoyable. Students learn by playing, collaborating, and competing in a supportive environment that fosters confidence and a love for math! 

Tournaments
Each year, the Math Pentathlon Institute organizes regional tournaments, where students from participating schools come together to compete and showcase their skills in a supportive and exciting environment.

More information can be found at https://www.mathpentath.org/
